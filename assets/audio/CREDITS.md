# Audio sources

Water, splash and bubble recordings: **rubberduck**, “40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX”, 29 July 2018.

- Original page and license declaration: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
- Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/water-splash-slime-sfx.zip
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Selected original OGG files are preserved in source/. WAV outputs use onset trimming, short fades and loudness normalization. The quiet ambient layer is a low-pass filtered water loop; the previous synthetic melody is removed. scripts/generate-audio.py reproduces the edits with FFmpeg. No synthetic oscillator layers remain. Perceptual approval remains the user's judgment; peak/loudness checks alone cannot certify good sound design.
